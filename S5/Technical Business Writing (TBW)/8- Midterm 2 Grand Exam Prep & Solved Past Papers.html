1The Case Study Master Lab: Diagnosing Technical Communication Scenarios

How to dissect FAST-NUCES exam case studies, classify data sources, and recommend professional remedies

The Anatomy of a FAST Case Study Question

In every recent Midterm 2 exam, FAST examiners provide a realistic scenario involving an engineering team, software company, or corporate research group. The questions test three specific competencies:

  1. Identifying Data Types Collected: Classifying research activities into Primary vs. Secondary and Qualitative vs. Quantitative.
  2. Spotting Document Design & Organizational Flaws: Identifying failures in visual hierarchy, lack of executive summaries, poor heading structure, or confusing raw data with analytical interpretation.
  3. Prescribing Professional Remedies: Recommending specific strategies (e.g., criteria-based body organization, 3-level heading hierarchy, visual lead-in statements, executive summaries) to make reports presentation-ready for C-level executives.
Solved Case Study 1: Tech Nova Analytics & Green Energy Pakistan (Fall 2025 Sessional II) 15 Marks

Scenario: "Tech Nova Analytics, a fast-growing data science startup, was contracted by Green Energy Pakistan (GEP) to research innovative ways to reduce electricity consumption in corporate buildings using AI. The team collected: (1) energy audits conducted in five corporate offices, (2) structured interviews with facility managers and IT administrators, (3) an online survey measuring employee energy-use behavior, and (4) information from published academic studies and industry reports on smart energy systems. When compiled, the initial report had severe flaws: it lacked clear logical organization, had inconsistent formatting (fonts, headings, tables varied), failed to distinguish between raw data and analysis, and omitted charts/diagrams for non-technical stakeholders."

Part (A) Solution: Classification of Data Sources Collected (10 Marks)

Tech Nova collected four distinct data streams that allowed a multi-dimensional understanding of energy consumption through methodological triangulation:

  • 1. Corporate Energy Audits (Primary, Quantitative / Observational Data): Direct empirical measurements of kilowatt-hour consumption, peak load hours, and equipment power draws across five physical offices. This established an objective baseline of physical energy waste without subjective bias.
  • 2. Structured Interviews with Facility & IT Managers (Primary, Qualitative Data): First-hand expert insights regarding technical HVAC operations, server cooling constraints, and organizational friction. Provides qualitative depth explaining *why* certain energy-saving protocols fail.
  • 3. Online Employee Surveys (Primary, Quantitative Data): Numerical data captured using structured Likert-scale questions regarding employee habits (e.g., workstation power-down frequency, thermostat settings). Measures organizational behavioral patterns across a broad statistical sample.
  • 4. Published Academic Studies & Industry Reports (Secondary Data): Pre-existing, peer-reviewed benchmarks and smart energy algorithms published by external researchers. Provides authoritative theoretical models and comparative industry standards to validate Tech Nova's AI predictions.
Part (B) Solution: Diagnosis of Report Flaws & Recommended Strategies (5 Marks)

Two Deficiencies in the Initial Report:

  1. Failure to Distinguish Raw Data from Analysis: Dumping unprocessed audit numbers alongside interpretive text creates cognitive overload for C-level executives who lack the time to calculate implications themselves.
  2. Absence of Visual Aids & Inconsistent Typography: Presenting numerical trends in dense textual blocks without charts, combined with erratic fonts and heading styles, severely impairs professional credibility and scanability.

Two Specific Remediation Strategies:

  1. Implement a 3-Tier Macro Structure with an Executive Summary: Front-load the document with a 1-page Executive Summary highlighting the core ROI (e.g., 22% potential energy savings via AI scheduling). Relegate massive raw audit spreadsheets and complete survey questionnaires to Appendices, keeping the Body focused strictly on high-level criteria-based analysis.
  2. Deploy Standardized Visual Aids with the 3-Step Integration Rule: Convert complex audit logs into clear, labeled figures (e.g., bar charts comparing hourly peak energy draws and visual floorplan heatmaps). Ensure every visual follows the three-step sequence: introduce with a lead-in sentence, present the labeled chart, and immediately interpret the strategic business implication in the text below.
Solved Case Study 2: CodeMatrix Solutions (Spring 2025 Sessional II) 5 Marks

Scenario: "CodeMatrix Solutions, a mid-sized software house in Karachi, initiated a research project to improve employee productivity and client satisfaction using a mixed-method approach. The company conducted surveys with Likert-scale questions distributed to 80 employees and 50 clients. To complement this, in-depth interviews and focus group discussions were conducted with Project Managers and senior developers regarding workflow challenges, motivation, and communication gaps."

Question: What were the types of data collected during the research that allowed CodeMatrix Solutions to see the full picture and bring improvements?

CodeMatrix Solutions deployed a Mixed-Method Primary Research Design combining:

  • 1. Primary Quantitative Data (Surveys with Likert-Scale Questions): Captured standardized, numerical ratings on productivity, communication response times, and client delivery satisfaction from 80 employees and 50 clients. This allowed statistical aggregation, percentage calculations, and trend benchmarking.
  • 2. Primary Qualitative Data (In-Depth Interviews & Focus Groups): Captured rich, explanatory verbal narratives and contextual perceptions from key stakeholders (Project Managers and senior developers). This uncovered underlying root causes of friction—such as unclear specification changes, estimation inaccuracies, and meeting fatigue—that numerical survey scores alone could never explain.
  • Methodological Triangulation: By cross-verifying quantitative survey metrics against qualitative focus group insights, management eliminated subjective bias and gained an accurate, 360-degree operational picture.

2Survey Questionnaire Design & IEEE Citation Mastery

Pfeiffer's rules for primary survey construction, question types, and IEEE technical documentation

Definition
Primary Research Data: Original information gathered first-hand directly from the real world to answer a specific research question (e.g., surveys, interviews, direct observation, lab experiments).
Secondary Research Data: Information gathered, analyzed, and published by someone else for another primary purpose (e.g., peer-reviewed journals, trade magazines, textbooks, government white papers).

Where Do Secondary Sources Appear in a Formal Report? (Fall 2024 Exam Question)

In a Formal Report, secondary research sources are utilized primarily in three specific sections:

  1. Introduction / Background: Establishing the historical context, defining industry standards, and demonstrating the importance of the problem.
  2. Literature Review / State-of-the-Art: Synthesizing prior academic and industrial research to show what solutions have already been attempted and identifying the gaps your report addresses.
  3. References / Bibliography Page: The formal inventory at the end of the report where every cited secondary source is listed in full according to IEEE formatting standards.

IEEE Referencing & Citation Rules (Fall 2020 Past Paper)

FAST-NUCES follows the standard **IEEE (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers)** citation format in technical reports:

  • In-Text Citations: Citations appear as sequential numbers enclosed in square brackets on the line of text, inside punctuation:
    • Single source: "Recent advances in natural language processing have enabled automated essay scoring [1]."
    • Multiple sources: "Several algorithms have demonstrated high classification accuracy [2], [4]–[6]."
  • Reference List (End Matter): Listed in numerical order of their appearance in the text (NOT alphabetically!).
The 4 Standard IEEE Reference Templates

1. Journal Article Template:

[1] J. K. Author, "Title of article," *Title of Journal*, vol. x, no. x, pp. xxx–xxx, Month, Year.

Example: [1] M.-H. Huang and R. T. Rust, "Artificial intelligence in service," Journal of Service Research, vol. 21, no. 2, pp. 155–172, May, 2018.

2. Conference Paper Template:

[2] J. K. Author, "Title of paper," in *Proc. Name of Conf.*, City, State/Country, Year, pp. xxx–xxx.

Example: [2] E. Brynjolfsson and A. McAfee, "The business of artificial intelligence," in Proc. IEEE Int. Conf. Data Eng., San Diego, CA, USA, 2020, pp. 45–52.

3. Book Template:

[3] J. K. Author, *Title of Book*, xth ed. City of Publisher, Country: Publisher, Year.

Example: [3] W. S. Pfeiffer, Technical Communication: A Practical Approach, 8th ed. Boston, MA, USA: Pearson, 2013.

4. Online Report / Website Template:

[4] J. K. Author or Organization, "Title of document," Organization, City, Country, Report No., Year. [Online]. Available: URL. [Accessed: Month Day, Year].

3Abstract & Executive Summary Mastery: The De-Scrambler Lab

The 5 canonical abstract components and the complete solved Spring 2025 sentence-ordering exam drill

Definition
Abstract / Executive Summary: A concise, standalone condensation of a formal document that summarizes the research context, purpose, methodology, key findings, and practical conclusions. It enables busy executives and technical professionals to understand the core value of a study without reading the full report.

Why Is an Abstract / Executive Summary Essential in a Formal Report? (Spring 2025 - 5 Marks)

An Abstract / Executive Summary is considered indispensable for four core reasons:

  1. Accommodates Executive Decision-Makers: High-level executives, directors, and clients rarely have time to read 30+ pages of granular data. The summary delivers the bottom-line business and financial implications in 1 to 2 minutes.
  2. Facilitates Rapid Document Indexing & Screening: In corporate archives and digital academic databases, researchers use the abstract to determine whether the report is relevant to their specific project before retrieving the full document.
  3. Provides a Mental Framework for Technical Specialists: Even engineers reading the full report benefit from reading the abstract first; it establishes an overarching cognitive roadmap of how the experiments, data, and conclusions interlock.
  4. Ensures Standalone Utility: A properly written abstract is completely self-contained. It makes coherent sense when circulated as an independent document to board members or stakeholders.

The 5 Canonical Components of an Abstract

Component # & Name Core Question It Answers Typical Language Signals & Keywords
1. Background / Context What is the broad real-world situation or problem domain? "In recent years...", "Modern organizations face...", "With the rapid expansion of..."
2. Purpose / Objective What exact goal did this specific project aim to achieve? "The objective of this project was to...", "This paper explores...", "We investigated..."
3. Methodology / Approach How was the investigation or technical development conducted? "Using machine learning algorithms...", "A qualitative survey of 120 users was deployed...", "Data was gathered via..."
4. Key Results / Findings What specific numerical or qualitative discoveries were made? "The system achieved an accuracy rate of 92%...", "Results indicate a 35% reduction in...", "Analysis revealed..."
5. Conclusion / Implications What do these results mean for the industry or future practice? "These findings demonstrate the potential of...", "We recommend implementing...", "The study confirms that..."
The Spring 2025 Solved Abstract De-Scrambler Exam Question 5 Marks

Exam Prompt: "Using the sample abstract provided, identify its key parts and arrange them in the correct order. You may mention the number in the correct order along with the name of each section of the abstract.
Title: Enhancing Strategic Decision-Making through AI-Powered Data Analytics"

GIVEN SCRAMBLED SENTENCES: (1) Using machine learning algorithms and advanced data visualization tools, the system identifies patterns and trends that would otherwise remain hidden. (2) These results demonstrate the potential of combining AI with data analytics to drive efficiency and innovation across various industries. (3) This project explores the integration of data analytics and artificial intelligence (AI) to enhance business decision-making processes. (4) The project achieved an accuracy rate of 92% in predicting customer behavior and significantly reduced decision-making time by 35%. (5) The objective was to design a predictive analytics model capable of analyzing large datasets and generating real-time insights to support strategic planning.
Official Step-by-Step De-Scrambling Solution:
Step # in Standard Order Sentence # Identified Component Name Analytical Justification
1st Sentence (3) Background / Context Establishes the broad problem domain: "This project explores the integration of data analytics and AI to enhance business decision-making..."
2nd Sentence (5) Purpose / Objective States the specific project goal: "The objective was to design a predictive analytics model capable of analyzing large datasets..."
3rd Sentence (1) Methodology / Approach Explains technical tools and methods used: "Using machine learning algorithms and advanced data visualization tools, the system identifies patterns..."
4th Sentence (4) Key Results / Findings Delivers empirical metrics: "The project achieved an accuracy rate of 92%... and reduced decision-making time by 35%."
5th Sentence (2) Conclusion / Implications Synthesizes broad future impact: "These results demonstrate the potential of combining AI with data analytics to drive efficiency across various industries."

Final Ordered Sequence: (3) → (5) → (1) → (4) → (2)

MEMORANDUM TO: Dr. Amir Masood, Director of Student Affairs, FAST-NUCES Lahore FROM: Hamza Naveed, President, Student Academic Council DATE: November 2, 2024 SUBJECT: Proposal for Establishing a Student-Led Peer Tutoring Program at FAST Lahore ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── A. ABSTRACT To address rising academic attrition and improve student performance in foundational computing courses, the Student Academic Council proposes the establishment of an organized, volunteer-driven Peer Tutoring Program for Spring 2025. Educational research confirms that peer-assisted learning increases course pass rates by up to 24% while simultaneously fostering leadership skills among upper-class mentors. This proposal details the logistical framework, academic oversight, and minimal resource requirements (totaling 40,000 PKR for semester logistics) required to launch this high-impact student initiative. B. BODY 1. Program Justification & Empirical Need: An internal academic survey conducted in October 2024 revealed that 68% of sophomore students enrolled in Programming Fundamentals, Object-Oriented Programming (OOP), and Data Structures felt overwhelmed by programming lab assignments. Furthermore, departmental data shows that student-to-TA ratios exceed 45:1 during peak lab hours, leaving teaching assistants unable to provide personalized debugging support. Peer tutoring bridges this gap by offering non-judgmental, accessible, after-hours peer mentoring. 2. Operational Logistics & Administration: • Tutor Selection Criteria: Tutors will be recruited from junior and senior batches with a minimum CGPA of 3.4 and a grade of 'A' or 'A-' in the target course. Candidates will be vetted via a brief interview. • Scheduling & Venues: Tutoring sessions will run Monday through Thursday from 4:00 PM to 6:30 PM in reserved rooms CS-01 and CS-02. • Academic Oversight: A designated faculty advisor from the Department of Computer Science will approve weekly topic coverage and review tutor attendance logbooks. • Code of Conduct: Tutors will focus strictly on conceptual explanations, algorithm tracing, and syntax guidance; writing direct assignment code for tutees is strictly prohibited under university plagiarism policies. 3. Implementation Timeline: • Weeks 1–2: Tutor recruitment drive, screening, and 2-hour orientation workshop. • Weeks 3–12: Active tutoring clinics; bi-weekly progress review by Student Affairs. • Weeks 13–14: Student feedback evaluation survey and final semester reporting. 4. Budget & Resource Allocation: • Room Reservations & Utilities: Existing campus facilities (0 PKR). • Printed Diagnostic Worksheets & Marketing Flyers: 15,000 PKR. • Semester-End Volunteer Appreciation Shields & Certificates: 25,000 PKR. • Total Proposed Budget: 40,000 PKR. C. CONCLUSION Establishing a structured peer tutoring program will enhance student retention, reduce probation rates, and cultivate a culture of academic solidarity at FAST Lahore with minimal university expenditure. We respectfully request your authorization by November 20, 2024, to enable tutor recruitment before final exams.

Q2 [10 Marks]: Short Answer Questions

Q2 (1) Solution: Researching Existing Peer Tutoring Programs

(i) To compare student satisfaction and teachers' views: Collect Primary Qualitative Data utilizing structured interviews with faculty coordinators and focus group discussions with participating tutees to gather nuanced subjective feedback on program atmosphere and instructional quality.

(ii) To analyze success rates and dropout rates: Collect Secondary Quantitative Data from institutional registrar records and academic audit databases to analyze objective numerical trends, such as historical GPA shifts, course retention percentages, and probation reduction rates.

Q2 (2) Solution: Secondary Data Sources in a Formal Report

Definition: Secondary data sources consist of information, statistics, and findings that were previously collected, analyzed, and published by third-party researchers for an earlier primary purpose.

Three Concrete Examples: (1) Peer-reviewed academic journals (e.g., IEEE Transactions), (2) Government and industry statistical handbooks (e.g., Pakistan Bureau of Statistics census reports), (3) Authoritative trade publications and company technical whitepapers.

Section of a Formal Report: Secondary sources are used primarily in the Introduction, Background, and Literature Review sections to establish the theoretical context and validate the project against established industry benchmarks, and are cataloged in the References section.
